# 👨‍💻 About Me

Hello, I'm **Hussain**, an IT professional passionate about IT Service Delivery Management, IT operations, cybersecurity, and continuous professional development.

My professional interests extend across two complementary areas: **IT Service Delivery Management and Cybersecurity**, with a particular ambition to develop into a Security Operations Center (SOC) Analyst.

I believe that understanding how IT environments operate is an important foundation for understanding how to protect them.

My background and interests in IT service management have encouraged me to explore the security side of technology, including how organizations monitor their infrastructure, detect suspicious activity, investigate security incidents, and protect business-critical systems.

I started building this portfolio to document my professional growth, strengthen my technical knowledge, and gain practical experience through hands-on projects, threat hunts, investigations, detection development, and IT service management exercises.

I want this repository to be more than a collection of notes. It will be a practical demonstration of what I learn, what I build, how I investigate problems, and how I continuously improve my capabilities.

## 🎯 Why I Started This Journey

I started this journey because I want to expand my knowledge beyond traditional IT operations and develop the technical skills required to work in cybersecurity.

I am particularly interested in understanding:

- How attackers compromise systems and networks.
- How security teams identify suspicious behavior.
- How SIEM platforms collect and analyze security events.
- How analysts investigate alerts and determine whether activity is malicious.
- How threat hunting can uncover activity that automated alerts may miss.
- How security incidents are investigated, documented, and remediated.
- How IT operations and cybersecurity teams work together to protect organizations.

I also want to strengthen my IT Service Delivery Management capabilities, particularly in service governance, incident management, SLA performance, stakeholder communication, vendor management, and operational excellence.

My objective is to combine operational understanding with hands-on cybersecurity skills.

## 💡 My Professional Philosophy

I believe successful technology management requires more than technical knowledge.

It requires understanding the business, managing risk, maintaining service availability, protecting information, communicating effectively, and continuously improving processes.

IT Service Delivery and Cybersecurity are closely connected.

Reliable IT services depend on secure infrastructure, effective incident response, strong operational controls, and collaboration between technology and security teams.

My goal is to develop expertise across both disciplines and build a well-rounded professional profile.

# 🔐 CYBERSECURITY & SOC ANALYST PORTFOLIO

This section represents my practical learning journey toward becoming a SOC Analyst.

My objective is to develop the ability to identify suspicious activities, analyze security events, investigate potential threats, and document findings using industry-recognized security frameworks.

## 🔍 Threat Hunting

Each threat hunt will follow a structured investigation methodology.

**Hunt documentation template:**

- **Technique:** MITRE ATT&CK ID and technique name.
- **Objective:** What activity am I trying to identify?
- **Summary:** How attackers use this technique.
- **Data Sources:** Windows Event Logs, Sysmon, SIEM telemetry, network logs.
- **Detection Strategy:** Suspicious indicators and behavioral patterns.
- **KQL/SPL:** Queries used during the investigation.
- **Findings:** Results and observations.
- **Triage & Investigation:** How to validate suspicious activity.
- **MITRE ATT&CK Mapping:** Relevant tactics and techniques.
- **Mitigation:** Recommended defensive actions.
- **Lessons Learned:** Improvements and additional detection opportunities.

## 💻 Detection Engineering

I will develop and document reusable security detection queries.

Each detection should include:

- Detection objective.
- MITRE ATT&CK mapping.
- Log sources and required fields.
- Suspicious indicators.
- KQL or SPL query.
- Expected query results.
- False-positive considerations.
- Investigation recommendations.
- Severity and response guidance.

## 🧾 Security Incident Investigations

Investigation reports will demonstrate how I approach potential security incidents.

Each report should include:

- Case ID.
- Incident summary.
- Alert details.
- Affected users and endpoints.
- Timeline of events.
- Relevant security logs.
- Indicators of Compromise (IOCs), where applicable.
- Investigation queries.
- MITRE ATT&CK mapping.
- Findings and conclusions.
- Containment and remediation recommendations.
- Lessons learned.

All investigations will distinguish between confirmed malicious activity, suspicious activity, and benign findings.

## 🎯 MITRE ATT&CK Framework

I will use MITRE ATT&CK to understand attacker behavior and map security investigations to recognized adversary tactics and techniques.

Areas of interest include:

- Initial Access.
- Execution.
- Persistence.
- Privilege Escalation.
- Defense Evasion.
- Credential Access.
- Discovery.
- Lateral Movement.
- Collection.
- Command and Control.
- Exfiltration.
- Impact.

## 🖥️ SIEM & Security Monitoring

My hands-on learning will focus on:

- Microsoft Sentinel.

- Splunk.
- KQL query development.
- SPL query development.
- Windows Security Event Logs.
